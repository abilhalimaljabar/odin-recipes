Air Fryer French Fries

Air fryer french fries use very little oil but still come out crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, which makes them a great alternative to the deep fried ones without the heavy greasiness that usually comes with it.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound russet potatoes, peeled
  • 2 teaspoons vegetable oil
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

Directions

  1. Steps 1
    Gather all ingredients.

  2. Steps 2
    Cut each potato lengthwise into ⅜-inch-thick slices; cut slices into ⅜-inch-wide sticks. Place potato sticks in a bowl of cold water; soak to release excess starches, about 5 minutes. Drain.

  3. Steps 3
    Pour boiling water over potatoes until covered by a few inches; set aside for 10 minutes. Drain potatoes; transfer to some paper towels. Blot excess water from potatoes; cool completely, at least 10 minutes.

  4. Steps 4
    Place cooled potatoes in a bowl; drizzle with oil. Season with cayenne; toss to coat.

  5. Steps 5
    Preheat an air fryer to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C) according to manufacturer's instructions.

  6. Steps 6
    Stack potatoes in a double layer in the air fryer basket.

  7. Steps 7
    Air fry for 15 minutes. Slide basket out; toss fries. Continue air frying until golden brown, about 10 minutes more. Transfer fries to a bowl; toss with salt. Serve immediately.
